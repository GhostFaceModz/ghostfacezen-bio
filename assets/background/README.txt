VIDEO BACKGROUND SLOT
Drop your own licensed background.webm or background.mp4 into this folder.
Uncomment the <video id="background-video"> hook in index.html and match the source filename/type.
Recommended: 1080p or lower, 10–20 second seamless loop, no sound, under 8 MB.
The video is muted, begins after click-to-enter, and pauses when motion is off, the page is hidden, or reduced motion is requested. CSS atmosphere stays as the fallback.
